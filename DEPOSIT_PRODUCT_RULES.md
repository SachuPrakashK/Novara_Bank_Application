# Deposit Product Rules

V7 supplies two explicitly labelled INR sandbox products: one fixed and one monthly recurring product. Amount/tenure bounds, calculation method, rate version, grace period, premature-closure permission, renewal permission, and effective dates are server-authoritative.

Only `CREDIT_LINKED_ACCOUNT` is accepted as a maturity instruction. Premature closure and renewal are disabled because no approved penalty, repricing, renewal, or holiday policy was supplied. Historical contracts snapshot the product/rate policy accepted at confirmation.
