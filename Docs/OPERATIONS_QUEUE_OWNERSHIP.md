# Operations Queue Ownership

Status: **PROPOSED — PENDING CUSTOMER OPERATIONS APPROVAL**

Proposed categories follow product/request classification, but final departments and commitments are not invented. Each queue needs a named business owner, supervisor group, membership source, supported request categories, organizational scope, hours/continuity plan, SLA policy, and escalation destination.

Assignment options are pull/claim, rules-based distribution, or supervisor assignment. Recommendation: policy-selected hybrid with atomic claim, workload limits, skill/scope validation, deterministic unassigned handling, and supervisor-controlled reassignment. Restricted queues must not be globally visible.

Metrics may include unassigned count, age percentiles, approaching/breached SLA, workload distribution, reassignments, and resolution outcomes. Definitions, targets, pause rules, holidays, and alert thresholds remain pending. Continuity requires secondary ownership, documented handover, provider outage procedures, and tested recovery—not shared accounts.
